Privacy Policy

How we protect and manage your personal information at Samyuktha Travels

Last Updated: June 22, 2026

1. Overview

Samyuktha Travels ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details the types of personal data we collect when you use our website, mobile application, and booking platform, and how we protect and process that data.

2. Information We Collect

When you book a cab or interact with our services, we collect information necessary to facilitate your travel plans, including:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, phone number, and email address.
  • Booking Details: Travel date, departure city, destination city, vehicle type preference, and special instructions.
  • Real-time Location: Driver location tracking updates to provide accurate arrival estimates.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following specific purposes:

  • To register, process, and confirm your taxi booking requests.
  • To send automated booking alerts and status updates to you and our administration team via WhatsApp.
  • To assign designated drivers to your trip and share relevant pickup details.
  • To improve website performance, security, and customer experience.

4. Data Security & Storage

We implement appropriate physical, technical, and administrative security measures to protect your personal details against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. Your information is securely stored inside our database infrastructure and is only accessible by authorized administrators.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We strictly do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties. We share limited customer information only with the driver assigned to your booking to coordinate your travel. WhatsApp alerts are securely routed via our backend messaging bot service.
